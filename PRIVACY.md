# Privacy

Study Desk is a set of instructions (skills) for Claude. This page says what that means for your data.

## What Study Desk collects

Nothing. The plugin contains no code that runs for you, no server and no tracking. Its author receives no data from your use of it: not your notes, not your answers, not your conversations, not the fact that you used it.

## What it reads

When you use a skill, Claude reads what you put into the conversation: your notes, slides, chapters, past papers and the answers you type. Study Desk only tells Claude how to work with them.

## Where your data goes

- Your conversation, including anything you paste, is handled by Anthropic under the terms and privacy policy of the Claude product you are using. Study Desk does not change that and adds no other recipient.
- The skills instruct Claude never to put your material into a web search.
- No skill writes a file unless you ask for one. The flashcard block is text you copy yourself into Anki or wherever you keep cards.

## What you can do

- Paste only the material you want worked on; a chapter at a time is enough.
- Delete a conversation in Claude to remove it, under the terms of your Claude product.
